Questions before you start

Good to
know.

Quick answers about classes, timings and your first visit. Anything else, just call the academy.

Do I need martial arts experience to join?

No. Korean Martial Arts Academy Nepal welcomes every level. Complete beginners train alongside experienced students, and every class starts from clear instruction and strong fundamentals.

Where is the academy?

The academy is at Bishal Chowk in Lalitpur, Nepal, in the Kathmandu Valley. Open the location in Google Maps ↗

What are the class timings?

Taekwondo runs 5:00–6:00 AM and 6:00–7:00 AM in the morning, and 4:00–5:00 PM and 5:00–6:00 PM in the evening. Muay Thai runs 7:00–8:30 AM and 6:00–7:20 PM. Call 985-1233728 to confirm kickboxing sessions.

Who teaches the classes?

Taekwondo is taught by Master Raju Shrestha, a 5th Dan black belt. Muay Thai and kickboxing are coached by Santosh Ghalan, who focuses on complete striking, pad work, footwork and conditioning.

What is the difference between Muay Thai, kickboxing and Taekwondo?

Muay Thai, the “art of eight limbs”, uses punches, kicks, knees and elbows, with pad work and conditioning. Kickboxing focuses on punch and kick combinations, footwork and timing. Taekwondo is a Korean martial art known for fast, dynamic kicks and forms, and builds flexibility, focus and discipline.

How do I book a trial class?

Call the academy on 985-1233728. The coaches will help you choose the right discipline and session, and then your first step is simply showing up.
